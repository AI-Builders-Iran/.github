# 🧠 About Us

**AI Builders** is an open and collaborative AI engineering community where passionate students, researchers, and developers build practical Artificial Intelligence solutions together.

We believe that real learning begins when knowledge meets implementation.

Instead of learning AI only through tutorials and courses, our members collaborate on complete engineering projects that reflect real industry workflows—from data collection to deployment.

Every project becomes an opportunity to learn, contribute, review code, solve problems, and grow as an AI engineer.

---

# 🌍 Our Vision

To build one of the most active AI engineering communities where learning happens through collaboration, open-source development, and real-world problem solving.

We aim to help aspiring AI engineers gain practical experience while contributing to impactful projects.

---

# 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is simple:

- 🚀 Build real AI products
- 🤝 Learn through collaboration
- 📚 Share knowledge openly
- 🌍 Contribute to open source
- 💼 Prepare members for industry
- 🏆 Participate in competitions and research
- 📈 Continuously improve engineering skills

# 🔬 What We Build

Our projects span multiple areas of Artificial Intelligence, including:

# ⚙️ Engineering Workflow

Every project follows a collaborative engineering workflow similar to professional AI teams.

```text
Problem Definition
        │
        ▼
 Dataset Collection
        │
        ▼
 Data Annotation
        │
        ▼
Data Cleaning & Analysis
        │
        ▼
 Feature Engineering
        │
        ▼
 Model Development
        │
        ▼
 Training & Fine-tuning
        │
        ▼
 Evaluation
        │
        ▼
 Optimization
        │
        ▼
 API Development
        │
        ▼
 Deployment
        │
        ▼
 Documentation
```

Every member can contribute to one or more stages depending on their interests and expertise.
