# 🧠 About

**AI Builders Iran** is an open and collaborative community focused on developing real-world Artificial Intelligence projects.

We believe the best way to learn AI is by **building**, **collaborating**, and **sharing knowledge**.

Rather than only watching tutorials, our members work together on practical projects that simulate professional AI development workflows.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced AI engineer, if you're passionate about learning and contributing, you're welcome here.

- 🚀 Build production-ready AI projects
- 🤝 Learn through teamwork
- 📚 Improve technical and research skills
- 🌍 Contribute to open-source projects
